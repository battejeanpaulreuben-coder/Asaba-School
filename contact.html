Admission Process

Joining Asaba School is simple. Here's how to get started.

1️⃣

Submit Enquiry

Fill in the form on this page or call us directly. Tell us about your child and the class you're interested in.

2️⃣

Visit the School

Come and see the school in person. Meet our teachers, tour the classrooms, and get answers to your questions.

3️⃣

Complete Registration

Fill in the official registration form at the school office and submit the required documents.

4️⃣

Pay & Begin

Pay the admission and term fees, collect uniform and stationery requirements, and your child is ready to start!

📋 Documents Required for Admission

  • ✅ Passport-size photograph of child (2 copies)
  • ✅ Birth certificate or baptism card
  • ✅ Previous school report card (if applicable)
  • ✅ Immunisation / health card
  • ✅ Parent/guardian National ID copy
  • ✅ Completed registration form
