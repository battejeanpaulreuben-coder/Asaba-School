Our Programs

From Nursery to Primary 7 — a seamless journey of learning and growth tailored to each child's stage of development.

🌸

Nursery Section

Ages 3 – 5 years

Our nursery program provides a warm, play-based learning environment that lays the essential foundations for a child's academic journey.

  • Baby Class (Age 3)
  • Middle Class (Age 4)
  • Top Class (Age 5)
  • Phonics & early literacy
  • Number recognition & counting
  • Creative arts & music
  • Social skills development
📖

Lower Primary

Primary 1 – Primary 3 (Ages 6–9)

Building core literacy and numeracy skills within the Uganda National Curriculum, with a focus on curiosity and foundational knowledge.

  • English Language & Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Science & Technology
  • Social Studies
  • Religious Education
  • Local Language (Runyoro)
  • Creative Arts & Physical Education
🎓

Upper Primary & PLE

Primary 4 – Primary 7 (Ages 10–13)

Comprehensive preparation for the Uganda National Examinations Board Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) with targeted revision programmes.

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Studies & Religious Education
  • Mock Examination Series
  • Past Paper Practice
  • Individual Pupil Mentorship

Our Curriculum Approach

We follow the Uganda National Curriculum, enriched with additional programs to develop well-rounded learners.

🇺🇬

National Curriculum

Fully aligned with the Uganda National Curriculum Framework and UNEB requirements for all classes.

💻

ICT Integration

Pupils are introduced to computers and digital literacy, preparing them for the modern world from an early age.

🌿

Environmental Education

Environmental awareness and sustainability are woven throughout our curriculum to nurture responsible citizens.

🙏

Values Education

Character formation, integrity, and respect are developed through Religious Education and daily school life.

Co-Curricular Activities

Education beyond the classroom — developing talent, teamwork, and character.

⚽

Sports

Football, netball, athletics, and volleyball. We compete at district level and beyond.

🎨

Arts & Craft

Drawing, painting, pottery, and craftwork that nurture creativity and self-expression.

🎵

Music & Drama

Choir, traditional dance, and drama performances at school events and competitions.

📖

Reading Club

Our school library and reading club foster a lifelong love for books and learning.

🌿

Gardening Club

Pupils tend the school garden, learning about nature, agriculture, and responsibility.

🏛️

Debating Society

Developing public speaking, critical thinking, and confidence through structured debates.
