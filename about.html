Our Story

Asaba School Masindi was founded in 1990 with a simple but powerful vision: to provide every child in Masindi with access to quality education in a safe, nurturing environment. Over the past three and a half decades, we have grown from a small community school into one of Masindi's most respected learning institutions.

Located on Ashton Drive in the heart of Masindi Municipality, our school caters to children from Nursery through Primary Seven. We are proud to serve over 1,000 pupils from diverse backgrounds, all united by a shared commitment to learning, growth, and excellence.

Our alumni have gone on to excel in secondary schools across Uganda and to build successful careers — a testament to the strong foundation Asaba provides. We continue to invest in our teachers, facilities, and curriculum to ensure every child we serve receives the best possible start in life.

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Asaba School Masindi.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating, inclusive, and values-centred education that equips every child with the knowledge, skills, and character needed to succeed in life and contribute positively to society.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the leading nursery and primary school in Masindi District, recognised for academic excellence, holistic child development, and community impact.

⭐

Our Motto

"Excellence Through Dedication" — a belief that with hard work, passion, and a supportive community, every child can achieve greatness.

Our Core Values

  • 🌟
    Excellence We set high standards and inspire every pupil and staff member to achieve their very best.
  • 🤝
    Integrity Honesty, respect, and ethical conduct are the foundation of our school community.
  • ❤️
    Care & Compassion Every child matters. We create a warm, inclusive environment where all pupils feel seen and valued.
